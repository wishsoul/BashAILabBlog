A functional check can verify an individual action without demonstrating that a person can complete the intended task.

This note records a change in Codex UX testing: the target moves from page-level functional coverage to coverage of the complete user-task loop. The useful question is not only whether controls work, but whether the journey reaches the business state the user intended.

This initial publication states the research direction. It does not claim a measured improvement or a completed comparative study.
